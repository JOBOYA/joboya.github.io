Support

Need a hand?

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, get in touch at the bottom of the page.

My phone doesn't say anything when I plug it in

  • Open the app and tap Hear a Sample. If you hear a voice, the app is fine and the automation needs a look.
  • In the app, open Setup and check that the reaction says Active. If it doesn't, follow the steps shown there again.
  • In the Shortcuts app, make sure the automation is turned on and set to run without asking.
  • In the app's Settings, check that reactions aren't paused, that Quiet Hours aren't on right now, and that Headphones Only is off.

Why do I have to set it up in Shortcuts?

iOS doesn't let apps watch the charger or the battery in the background. Automations in the Shortcuts app are the way Apple provides for this, and only you can create them. You do it once per reaction.

It talks even when my phone is on silent

That's expected. To play a voice while the app is closed, iOS requires an audio mode that ignores the silent switch. Use Pause Reactions or Quiet Hours in Settings to keep it quiet.

I bought the app and I have a new phone

Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account. Sign in with the same account on the new phone and download FeelMyPhone again from the App Store. You won't be charged again. You'll need to set up the automations once on the new phone.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
