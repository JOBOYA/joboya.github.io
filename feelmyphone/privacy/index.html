Privacy Policy

We don't collect your data.

Effective November 16, 2026

FeelMyPhone ("the app") is published by JOBOYA. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It makes no network requests of its own. We never see how you use it.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves your settings on your device so it can work: the character you picked, which reactions are on, your quiet hours, the volume, and the date each reaction first ran. This stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether the unlock is owned. We never receive your name, email address, or payment details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Shortcuts automations

Reactions are triggered by automations that you create in Apple's Shortcuts app. Those automations run on your device. The app only learns that one of its actions was asked to run.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
